## New research behind this round

| Finding | Source | What we did |
|---|---|---|
| Since Meta's "Andromeda" update, ads that look alike get treated as one ad. Small budgets do best with **3 clearly different angles** (problem, outcome, offer/proof), not 7–50 ads. | [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda-creative-diversification/), [Excite Media](https://www.excitemedia.com.au/meta-creative-diversification/) | 3 ads per ad set, each with its own angle |
| Holiday decorating causes **about 15,000 ER visits** each season. **Falls are the #1 cause** (34% of incidents). | [CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2014/CPSC-Estimates-More-Than-15000-Holiday-Decorating-Injuries-During-November-and-December) | H1, the "let us take the ladder" problem ad |
| Installers time early-booking offers around October deadlines (for example 15% off October installs, or 20% off before Nov 7). | [KDVR](https://digital-release.kdvr.com/?p=2367282), [KHOU](https://khou.com/article/entertainment/television/programs/great-day-houston/get-your-holiday-lights-handled-by-the-pros-at-abc-home-commercial-services/285-cc001139-d4a5-4e48-a39c-97e2df288c38) | H3 uses "Book in October". **No discount was invented.** Quinn decides whether to add one. |
| Full-service installers charge **$5–10/ft**. Labor-only is about $1.20–5/ft. | [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/seasonal/install-holiday-lighting) | R3 compares the kit against *full-service* pricing only, with the source cited |
| The competitor's longest-running ads (about 51 days) push "made to order" and "limited capacity". | Meta Ad Library (see proven-ads.md) | R1, "made to order in Maryland" |
| Labeled runs: no competitor claims this. | Ad Library search | R2 |
| A previous Heron ad targeted the **entire US** and spent $47.78 with no leads. | Our Ads Manager | Heron now targets only people who live within 15 miles of Towson |
