# Roofline Kit: Ad Launch Review (2026-10-08)

Quinn asked a group of three reviewers whether to run ads F and E at $5/day. Each reviewer was told to argue against the plan. Nothing is live. The campaign budget is set to $5/day and the campaign is paused.

## Verdict: not as it stands. Fix the store first, then run F only.

All three reviewers reached the same conclusion on their own:

| Reviewer | Verdict | Main reason |
|---|---|---|
| Budget and math | Go only with changes | $70 over 14 days buys about 25–45 visits and an expected 0.1–0.3 sales. Optimizing for Purchase with a pixel that has never recorded a sale teaches Meta nothing. |
| Creative and policy | **Don't run E. F only after fixes** | E shows a sensor box that may not ship with the kit, which is a "shown but not shipped" FTC risk. Its installer price comparison is only half-sourced. AI images will likely get Meta's "AI info" label. |
| Market and competitors | Not yet | $2.99/ft is the same price as an established store (Christmas Light Emporium, about $2.48–3.33/ft) and above Govee permanent lights (about $2/ft). Shoppers have no reason to trust the store: no reviews, AI photos, a myshopify URL, no guarantee. |

## What the numbers say
- **14 days at $5/day:** about 3,500 impressions, 35–60 clicks, 25–45 visits. Most likely 0 sales.
- **Competitor pricing:**

  | Option | Price |
  |---|---|
  | Christmas Light Emporium 100 ft kit | $333 |
  | Christmas Light Emporium 250 ft kit | $619 |
  | Govee 100 ft permanent lights | about $199 |
  | Wintergreen pre-lamped C9 | about $1/ft |
  | Local pro install | about $750–1,200 for a typical house |
- **Your cost to build** (stringer about $0.22/ft plus C9 LED bulbs about $0.67–1.56 each): about $1–1.90/ft. At $2.99/ft, that leaves roughly $1–2/ft before shipping and your labor.
- **Sales window:** about Oct 8 to Nov 15. That's the last realistic order date to build, ship and hang lights by Thanksgiving (Nov 26). Ad costs climb sharply in November.
- **Custom-cut plus labeled:** no direct competitor was found doing this. It's a real point of difference, but it's a convenience, not something people must have.

## Fix before spending $1
1. **Real photos.** One phone photo of a real labeled run, and one of a lit house. This is the biggest fix.
2. **Buy rooflinekit.com** ($16, available) and set it as the primary domain in Shopify.
3. **Test order and refund.** Confirm that PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart and Purchase all fire (Meta Events Manager > Test Events).
4. **Trust on the product page:**
   - A "Fits or we re-cut it free" guarantee
   - A warranty
   - Your name, face and phone number
   - An order-by date for Thanksgiving delivery
   - Shipping cost
5. **Confirm what ships.** If the kit has no sensor or timer, retire ad E's image. Reword the price line to: "Full-service installers often charge $5–10/ft every season. Do it yourself for $2.99/ft."

## Then launch like this
- **One ad (F) with real photos**, not two. All $5/day goes to one ad.
- **Objective: Traffic, optimizing for Landing Page Views,** not Purchase. Switch to Add to Cart and then Purchase once those events are firing.
- **Location: Mid-Atlantic and Northeast** (1–2 day ground shipping), not the whole US.
- **Judge it on cost per click, click-through rate and add-to-carts**, not sales.
- **Hard stop Nov 15.**

## Faster path to a first sale (do in parallel)
- Offer 2–3 Heron landscaping customers a roofline kit with optional install, at cost if needed. You get real photos, video and reviews that you can't fake.
- Film one install (measure, cut, label, clip, lit). Use it for Reels, YouTube and r/ChristmasLights.
