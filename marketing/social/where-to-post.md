# Where to Post: Ranked by How Much Money It Brings In

Researched 2026-10-06. Ranked by how likely each place is to produce a paying customer per hour of your time, not by follower counts. Read `safe-posting-rules.md` before posting anywhere.

## Heron Landscaping (local: Towson / Baltimore County)

### 1. Google Business Profile: free, highest value, do this first
When someone searches "Christmas light installation near me" or "landscaper Towson", Google shows 3 map listings above every website. If Heron isn't listed, you're invisible to the people who are actively looking.
- Set up at **business.google.com** as a service-area business. Hide your home address and set the service area to Towson plus Baltimore County.
- Add your services (holiday lighting install, fall cleanup, mowing, mulching), hours, phone, and real photos.
- Google mails or texts a verification code. It takes a few days, so start today.
- Then post weekly (offers and seasonal updates). Ask every happy customer for a Google review. Reviews are what move you into the top 3.
- Once it's verified, tell Claude. Zapier can post to it automatically, but you need to approve that connection yourself.

### 2. Local Facebook groups: free, fast, you post them (personal profile)
Search Facebook for each of these and join the ones with recent activity and more than 2,000 members:
- "Towson" / "Towson Community" / "Towson Neighbors"
- "Baltimore County" / "Baltimore County Neighbors"
- "Parkville", "Perry Hall", "Lutherville Timonium", "Rodgers Forge", "Loch Raven"
- "Baltimore Moms" (many homeowners; often has a weekly business-promo thread)

Rules: read each group's rules, wait 2–3 days after joining, post in 1 group per day, and use versions A/B/C from `safe-posting-rules.md`. **Best of all:** search each group for "Christmas lights" or "landscap" and answer anyone asking for a recommendation.

### 3. Nextdoor Business Page: free
- Create a free Business Page for Heron (business.nextdoor.com). Only promote from that, never from your personal account.
- Nextdoor search won't show you until you have **3 neighbor recommendations**. Ask your first 3 happy customers to recommend you there.
- On your personal account, only *reply* when a neighbor asks "who does Christmas lights?"

### 4. Facebook Page posts: automated (Claude)
- Already scheduled at 1 per day. It mostly builds trust for people who check you out after seeing a group post. Low reach on its own.
- **Blocked until you reconnect Zapier.**

### 5. Paper is underrated for a local service
- Door hangers in the 2–3 neighborhoods with the biggest rooflines (Rodgers Forge, Ruxton, Lutherville). A few hundred cost about $50–80 to print.
- Every house you light up is a billboard. Put a small yard sign at each install (ask the customer first).

### Skip for now
- **Angi, Thumbtack, Bark:** you pay per lead and compete with 5 other companies on the same lead. Not worth it until your reviews are strong.
- **Craigslist services:** heavy spam and few buyers. Low value.
- **r/baltimore:** must stay relevant to Baltimore and is hostile to ads. Only answer people asking for recommendations.

## Roofline Kit (online, nationwide)

### 1. r/ChristmasLights (about 46k members): high-intent DIYers
- People there are exactly the C9 roofline crowd. **Do not post ads.** Reddit communities ban drive-by self-promotion.
- Post your own real install (photos plus "how I did it") and answer measuring and C9 questions. Mention the kit only when someone asks or when it directly answers a question, and always say it's your product.
- This needs a real Reddit account with some history first. A brand-new account posting a link gets removed.

### 2. Facebook Page posts: automated (Claude)
- Already scheduled at 1 per day.

### 3. Pinterest: free, slow burn, perfect timing
- People plan Christmas lights on Pinterest in October and November. Pins of lit rooflines that link to your product page keep getting views for months.
- Use real photos when you have them. The hero product image works too.

### 4. Your own Heron installs: the best Roofline Kit content
- Film every lighting install you do for Heron. Those real videos are your Roofline Kit ads, posts and Reddit content. You get paid twice for the same job.
