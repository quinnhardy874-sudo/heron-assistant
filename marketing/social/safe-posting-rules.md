# Safe Posting Rules: How Not to Get Flagged

Researched 2026-10-06. Follow these for the Pages, groups and Nextdoor.

## What actually gets you flagged

| Risk | What happens | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Same promo posted in several groups quickly | Facebook detects matching text plus a matching link and gives a 24–48h posting block. Repeat offenses mean longer blocks or account closure. | **Max 1 group per day.** Use a different version (A/B/C below) each time. |
| Asking people to comment, tag, share, react or vote | Facebook demotes engagement bait, and repeat offenders get stricter demotions. | **Never** write "tag a friend", "comment below", "share this" or "drop a 👍". Asking for advice or recommendations is fine. |
| Breaking a group's own rules | An admin removes the post or bans you, and reports count against your account. | **Read each group's rules first.** Many only allow business posts on one day (like "Small Business Saturday"). |
| Promoting a business from your personal Nextdoor account | Nextdoor removes it. Business promotion must come from a free Business Page. | Make a free **Nextdoor Business Page** for Heron. On your personal account, only *answer* when someone asks for a recommendation. |
| Passing off AI images as your work | Not a ban, but if a neighbor spots it, you lose trust. Meta also auto-labels many AI images as "AI info". | AI images are only ever "what your house could look like." Never caption one as "our install." |
| Too many link posts with nothing else | Lower reach, and the Page looks like spam. | Page posts stay at 1 per day. Mix in posts without links once you have real job photos. |

## Page posting (automated, Zapier)
- 1 post per day at 10:55 AM ET, alternating Roofline Kit and Heron.
- Posting through Zapier uses Facebook's official API, so it's allowed and isn't treated as spam at 1 a day.
- If a Page post gets removed or a warning appears, stop the schedule and tell Claude.

## Group posting (manual, your personal profile)
Groups only allow posting from your personal profile, so this part has to be you. Start with Heron, since local groups want local services.

**Schedule:** 1 group per day, max 3 per week. Never the same group twice in 2 weeks.

**Groups to search on Facebook:** "Towson", "Towson Community", "Baltimore County", "Parkville", "Lutherville Timonium", "Perry Hall". Join them, read the rules, and wait 2–3 days after joining before posting.

**Best move: answer, don't advertise.** When someone asks "anyone know who does Christmas lights / fall cleanup?", reply right away. That's allowed everywhere and converts far better than a cold post.

### Version A (lighting)
Hi neighbors! I'm Quinn, I live locally and run Heron Landscaping. This year I'm also doing holiday light installs: design, hang, and takedown in January. If you want your roofline lit without climbing a ladder, text me at 410-830-0843 for a free quote. Thanks for letting me share!

### Version B (lighting)
Local guy here (Towson). I install Christmas lights on rooflines, gutters and peaks with clean straight lines, and I take them down after the holidays too. Happy to give anyone a quick free quote, just text 410-830-0843.

### Version C (fall cleanup)
Hey everyone, Quinn from Heron Landscaping here. I'm booking fall cleanups around Towson: leaves, beds, mulch and a final mow before winter. If anyone needs a hand, text 410-830-0843. Appreciate this group!

Use your own real photo in group posts if you have one. Never use the AI images in groups, because people will read them as your work.
